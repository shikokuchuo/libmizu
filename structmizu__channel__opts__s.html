Detailed Description

size-stamped options; mizu_channel_opts_init zeroes + stamps.

drop is the peer bootstrap payload, copied into the region at create (opaque bytes; its first byte is a MIZU_DROP_* tag by binding convention; the peer's binding reads them after attach).
