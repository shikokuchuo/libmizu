Detailed Description

One MIZL directory entry plus its validity-table pair (mizu.h documents the 32-byte entry): mizu_mizl_elem's out-param.

sexptype is the wire value — MIZU_MIZL_S4 is the S4 bit, the remainder a listed tag.
